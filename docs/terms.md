# Kanade usage information

Kanade plays local audio and includes an experimental, unofficial YouTube integration. The integration is not affiliated with YouTube or Google and may become unavailable if the service changes. YouTube content and use remain subject to the provider's terms: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms.

NewPipeExtractor is a GPL-3.0-or-later dependency; distribution of a build containing it must comply with its license. See https://github.com/TeamNewPipe/NewPipeExtractor.
